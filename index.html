🏃 Marathon Trainer

When is your marathon?

This app back-schedules the SWAP 12-Week Advanced Marathon Plan from your race date — 84 days of training ending on race day. Each day shows the planned workout, and you can journal how it went to track your progress.

Heads up: this plan is written for a Sunday race (rest days land on Mondays). Any date works — the whole plan just shifts to end on your race day.

The distance ranges create multiple plans in one — low end of the range for intermediate runners, high end for advanced athletes with a big base.

Already using this app on another device?

Enter the sync code from your other device (Settings → Cross-device sync) to restore your schedule and journal here.
